How it works

One useful weekly rhythm.

Capture context as work happens. Jetty turns what you saved into a short attention list for owner review.

Capture → save the client note and its source

Review → check who needs attention and why

Follow through → approve the next step and record the result

Privacy and control

Know where your business context goes.

Jetty keeps the context you save in your configured workspace. Model requests may be processed by the provider selected for your deployment.

In control.

Review what Jetty saved, export it, and remove it. Provider and retention details belong in the deployment record, not a blanket promise.
